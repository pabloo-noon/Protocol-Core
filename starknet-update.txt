Update on the Starknet side of the Noon bridge estate.

TAC and Sophon are now unenrolled on both Starknet warp routers. The closure of those two deprecated chains is complete at every end: Ethereum, zkSync, Base and HyperEVM point their TAC and Sophon remote tokens to a dead address with 1 wei limits, and Starknet no longer accepts or sends to them. Nothing can move between TAC or Sophon and any Noon chain anymore.

State after execution, read independently from the chain

Starknet routes and limits
- Starknet to Ethereum and Starknet to zkSync are the only live routes, for both USN and sUSN.
- On the Ethereum and zkSync side every Starknet route is rate limited since 1 October: 4,000,000 tokens per route per 8 hour sliding window, inbound and outbound, for USN and sUSN.

Still open on Starknet
- The sUSN router does not enroll HyperEVM, while our HyperEVM receiver lists Starknet. We will close that entry on HyperEVM so the configuration reads the same on both sides. No traffic has ever used that pair.
- Router ownership is a single-key account today. We are moving it to a multisig to match the rest of the estate, where every bridge contract sits behind a Safe or a timelock.
